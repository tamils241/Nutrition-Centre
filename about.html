Our Journey

A Growing 12-Year Story

From a single Green Park clinic to a pan-India coaching team.

2014

First Clinic Opens

Stackly starts as a two-desk practice in Green Park, New Delhi with a body-composition lab in the back room.

2017

Going Online

Video consultations begin and word-of-mouth spreads across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Pune in the first year.

2020

Clinical Care Division

A dedicated clinical wing for diabetes, thyroid, PCOS, kidney and cardiac cases with in-house lab partnerships.

2022

45+ Dietitians Nationwide

Our expert panel crosses 45 certified dietitians spanning 36 cities, plus sports and paediatric specialists.

2026

12,500+ Clients Cared For

Today Stackly blends body-composition science, blood-panel review and warm one-on-one coaching for 12,500+ members.
