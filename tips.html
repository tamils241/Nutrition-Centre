DAILY BASICS

8 Simple Rules for Better Eating.

You don't need a perfect diet. Start with a few consistent habits and build from there.

01

Build Balanced Plates

Include a useful combination of vegetables, protein, whole-food carbohydrates and healthy fats.

02

Prioritise Protein

Include protein-rich foods regularly across your meals to support satiety and everyday nutrition.

03

Add More Vegetables

Mix different colours and varieties of vegetables throughout the week.

04

Choose Whole Foods Often

Make minimally processed foods the foundation of most meals when practical.

05

Drink Enough Water

Keep water accessible throughout the day and use thirst as one useful hydration cue.

06

Slow Down While Eating

Give yourself time to enjoy your food and notice your hunger and fullness signals.

07

Plan Before You Get Hungry

Keep simple meal and snack options available so convenience does not always decide your choices.

08

Focus on Consistency

One imperfect meal does not undo your progress. Return to your routine at the next meal.

HYDRATION

Make Water the Easy Choice.

Hydration does not need to be complicated. Create an environment that makes drinking water easier.

01

Start Your Day

Keep a glass or bottle of water ready when you begin your morning routine.

02

Carry a Bottle

Having water nearby makes regular drinking more convenient during work and travel.

03

Pair It With Meals

Keep water available during meals and use it as your default drink more often.

04

Make It Interesting

If plain water feels repetitive, try adding natural flavours such as lemon or mint.

INDIAN MEAL GUIDE

Build a Better Indian Plate.

Healthy eating does not mean giving up familiar foods. Start by improving the balance and variety of your meals.

Vegetables

Add a generous variety of cooked or raw vegetables where practical.

Protein

Include options such as dal, beans, curd, paneer, eggs, fish or other preferred protein sources.

Carbohydrates

Choose rice, roti, millets, oats or other suitable carbohydrate foods according to your needs.

Healthy Fats

Use nuts, seeds and appropriate cooking oils thoughtfully as part of a balanced pattern.

ACTIVE LIFESTYLE

Fuel Your Workout Better.

Your food choices around activity can help you build a more consistent and enjoyable fitness routine.

BEFORE ACTIVITY

Keep It Practical

Choose familiar foods that suit your digestion and provide useful energy for your planned activity.

  • Easy-to-digest foods
  • Appropriate carbohydrates
  • Enough time before training
DURING ACTIVITY

Listen to Your Body

Hydration and nutrition needs can vary depending on exercise duration, intensity and environment.

  • Keep hydrated
  • Consider activity duration
  • Adjust to individual needs
AFTER ACTIVITY

Recover With Balance

A balanced meal or snack can help you return to your normal eating routine after training.

  • Include protein
  • Add carbohydrates as appropriate
  • Rehydrate regularly

MYTH VS REALITY

Let's Make Nutrition Less Confusing.

MYTH

Carbohydrates are always bad.

REALITY

Carbohydrates can be part of a balanced eating pattern. The type, portion and overall diet matter.

MYTH

Healthy eating must be expensive.

REALITY

Many nutritious foods such as dal, seasonal vegetables, fruits and grains can be affordable choices.

MYTH

You need to remove all your favourite foods.

REALITY

Sustainable nutrition can include flexibility and occasional favourite foods.

MYTH

One perfect meal creates a healthy diet.

REALITY

Overall eating patterns and consistency matter more than a single meal.
