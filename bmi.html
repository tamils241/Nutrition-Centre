Understanding BMI

What Is BMI?

A simple measurement used to compare body weight with height.

📐

Height & Weight

BMI uses your height and weight to produce a numerical value that can be placed into broad categories.

🔡

Simple Number

The result provides a quick way to begin a conversation about weight-related health factors.

📊

General Screening

BMI can be useful for screening at a population level, but it does not provide a complete picture of health.

🙍

Body Composition

BMI does not directly measure muscle mass, body fat distribution or overall body composition.

🎯

Useful Starting Point

Your BMI can be one piece of information when planning broader nutrition and lifestyle goals.

👨‍⚕️

Expert Interpretation

Individual circumstances should be considered when interpreting BMI and deciding on health goals.

kg/m² BMI Formula
The Formula

How Is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing body weight in kilograms by height in metres squared.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m) Example: weight divided by height in metres squared.

Important To Know

BMI Does Not Tell The Whole Story

Use BMI as one piece of information rather than a complete assessment of your health.

01

Muscle Mass

People with higher muscle mass may have a higher BMI without having high body fat.

02

Age & Context

Age, development and individual circumstances can affect how BMI should be interpreted.

03

Body Fat

BMI does not directly measure body fat percentage or where body fat is distributed.

04

Overall Health

Nutrition, activity, sleep, medical history and other factors also contribute to overall health.
